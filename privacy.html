The essentials

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. There are no in-app purchases, Firebase integration or online game saves. You can send privacy requests directly to KoalaPaprika.

01Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to Merge Dice (also named MergeDice) for iOS and Android and to this support website. The game is published by KoalaPaprika, the developer responsible for the personal information handled for the purposes described here. For privacy questions, contact luzashdev@gmail.com.

Merge Dice uses Google AdMob advertising. It has no in-app purchases, Firebase integration or online game-save service.

02Information handled

Game progress and settings

Game data saved on your device is used to run the game and retain your progress or preferences. KoalaPaprika does not provide an online backup of this data. Any device-level backup you enable is governed by your device or backup provider.

Advertising

Google AdMob processes information through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Depending on your operating system, permissions and consent, this includes your IP address and an approximate location inferred from it, advertising or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and technical diagnostics such as crashes and performance information. AdMob uses this information to deliver and measure ads, operate and improve its service, and detect fraud or abuse. Personalized advertising, where enabled and permitted, also uses this information to select relevant ads.

When you contact us

We receive your email address, any name you provide, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your question, investigate a problem or handle your privacy request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or unrelated sensitive information.

03Services and sharing

Google AdMob receives advertising-related information directly from the app and may share it with its service providers and advertising partners as described in Google's policies. Our email service, Gmail (Google), processes support correspondence. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. These services have their own privacy practices and controls.

04Advertising choices

Where consent is required, advertising processing that depends on consent must wait for that choice. You may refuse or withdraw consent; withdrawal does not affect processing that lawfully occurred beforehand. Use the advertising privacy choices presented in the app when available, or contact us for help finding or exercising your choices.

  • iOS: manage tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Permission is required before accessing the IDFA or tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. Apple's tracking controls.
  • Android: use your device's Google / Ads privacy settings to manage, reset or delete your advertising ID where supported. Menus vary by device and Android version. Google's privacy controls.

Restricting personalization or tracking does not necessarily remove advertising or all data processing: contextual ads, security and fraud prevention can still involve limited data, subject to applicable law and your choices. Device controls do not replace any separate in-app consent choices.

05Purposes and legal grounds

Where the GDPR or similar rules apply, we rely on consent for advertising identifiers and personalized advertising where required; our legitimate interests in responding to support requests, maintaining the service and preventing abuse for related support and security processing; and legal obligations where we must retain or disclose information. Local game data is used to provide the gameplay you request. Google's legal grounds for its own processing are explained in its privacy information.

06Retention and deletion

  • Local game data: remains on your device until removed. On Android, clearing the app's storage removes its local data. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading it keeps documents and data. Device backups may keep a separate copy. Removing game data can permanently erase your progress.
  • Support messages: are kept for the time needed to resolve your request and any related follow-up, then deleted unless a legal obligation or unresolved dispute requires retention. If an exception applies, retention is limited to that purpose.
  • Advertising information: follows Google's retention practices, which vary by data type and purpose. See Google's retention policy. Deleting the game does not automatically erase information already held by Google.

To request deletion of correspondence or other personal information held by KoalaPaprika, email luzashdev@gmail.com with the subject “Merge Dice — Privacy request”. We may ask for only the details needed to identify the relevant information and verify the request. For information controlled by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact us for guidance.

07Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Contact luzashdev@gmail.com. We respond within applicable legal time limits; under the GDPR this is generally one month, with an extension where legally permitted.

You may complain to your local data protection authority. In France, this is the CNIL. Where applicable US state law grants an opt-out from targeted advertising or a sale or sharing of personal information, you may exercise that right through the available advertising choices or by contacting us. We do not sell support correspondence; advertising disclosures may fall within broad local definitions of sharing.

08Security and international processing

Access to support correspondence is limited to handling support and privacy requests. This website uses HTTPS, and Google documents encryption in transit for data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google and GitHub may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Their policies describe the safeguards used for international transfers, including adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses where applicable. See Google's transfer information and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

09Children

Merge Dice is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete information in our control where appropriate. Additional age-related consent requirements may apply in your country.

10This website and updates

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics, advertising scripts, cookies or contact form. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the website, according to its Privacy Statement. Email links open your own email application.

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where required. Questions can be sent to luzashdev@gmail.com.
