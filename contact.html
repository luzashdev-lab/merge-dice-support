A few helpful answers

Privacy or data deletion requests

Email luzashdev@gmail.com with the subject “Merge Dice — Privacy request” and describe your request. To remove local game data, see retention and deletion. This can erase your progress. Information held by Google requires its own controls or request process.

Having a problem with an ad?

Tell us what happened, the approximate time and, if possible, the advertiser's name. A screenshot may help us identify the ad.

Is my progress saved online?

Merge Dice does not provide online game saves. Uninstalling the game or clearing its local data can cause you to lose progress. Any device backups depend on your settings and backup provider.

How are my messages used?

Your email address and the information you send are used to handle your request. Read our Privacy Policy for information about recipients, retention and your rights.
